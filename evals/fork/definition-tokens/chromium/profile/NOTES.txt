Two profiles of a whole-tree search for a string that is not in the timed query list.
Neither wall time is in chromium/summary.md.

strace.txt
  strace -c, main thread only. The workers are not attached, so the time is
  one futex. SPEEDREAD_STATS on that run: walks=1 entries=471431 stopped=0
  cancelled=0. The walk finished. Do not read the futex line as the cost model.

strace-follow.txt
  strace -f -c. Workers are attached. The tracer slowed the walk past the
  10 s deadline (stopped=1, 257492 entries). Syscall mix for that partial
  walk: futex, read, openat, statx, close, getdents64. Not a timing result.
